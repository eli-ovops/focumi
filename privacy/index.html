Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026

Overview

Focumi is a focus companion for iPhone. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information stored on your device

Focumi stores focus-session settings and progress locally on your device. If you choose apps or categories to limit during a focus session, that selection is stored locally to support the focus feature. Focumi does not send those selections to us.

Screen Time and Family Controls

Focumi asks for Apple Family Controls permission when you choose to use its focus-limiting feature. With your permission, Focumi uses Apple Screen Time controls to apply a Shield to the apps or categories you select during a focus session. You can change or remove the selection and stop a session in Focumi.

Notifications and Live Activities

If you allow notifications, Focumi may schedule local notifications for your focus session. Focumi may also show the current session in a Live Activity. These features use information on your device and do not send it to us.

Accounts, tracking, and sharing

Focumi does not provide an account sign-in feature. It does not include third-party analytics or advertising tracking in the current free version, and it does not sell your information.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app’s data practices change. The current version will be available on this page.
